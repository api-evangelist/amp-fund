# AMP Fund (AMP PBC)

> AMP is a public benefit corporation founded in 2025 by Anjney Midha, a former Andreessen Horowitz general partner, building an independent "AI grid" — a pooled compute utility for frontier AI research teams. AMP acts as an independent system operator rather than a cloud provider or a conventional venture firm: contributors supply underutilized GPU capacity to a shared network, and member research teams draw base-load and burst compute from that pool. AMP raised roughly $1.3 billion for its first fund and has committed a Public Wealth Fund of up to $500 million through 2030.
